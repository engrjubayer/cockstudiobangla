12+Years of Craft
Company Profile

A production house built by people who make things

Wooden Mart Communication started as a small fabrication workshop in Dhaka and has grown into a full production house serving events, exhibitions, branding, print and promotional-item needs for corporates, NGOs, government bodies and institutions across Bangladesh.

We keep design, fabrication and print in-house, which means fewer handoffs, tighter timelines, and materials that are checked by the same team from concept through install.
