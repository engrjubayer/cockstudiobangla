Company Overview

A single production house for every touchpoint of your brand

Wooden Mart Communication is a Dhaka-based production house serving corporates, government bodies, NGOs and institutions across Bangladesh. We plan events, fabricate exhibition environments, print at scale, and manufacture promotional items — all under one roof, so your brand stays consistent from concept to install.

  • In-house fabrication & printing
  • Dedicated account management
  • Pan-Bangladesh delivery & install
  • 12+ years of production experience
