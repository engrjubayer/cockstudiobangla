Frequently Asked Questions

Home/FAQ
What areas of Bangladesh do you serve?

We deliver and install nationwide, with our production facility based in Amin Bazar, Dhaka.

Do you handle both design and fabrication in-house?

Yes — design, fabrication and printing are all handled by our own team, which keeps timelines and quality consistent.

How far in advance should I request a quotation?

For standard projects we recommend at least 2–3 weeks lead time; large exhibitions or events may need 4–6 weeks.

Can you work within a fixed budget?

Yes, share your budget range in the quotation form and we'll propose options that fit it.

Do you offer reusable exhibition stall designs?

Many of our stall builds are modular and can be reused or reconfigured for future shows at reduced cost.

How do I submit files for a print job?

You can upload files directly through our Request Quotation form, or email them once a project is confirmed.

Still have questions?

Our team is happy to walk through your project in detail.
